About us

We build the system. You run the business.

PoppyPages Marketing is a small team that builds and runs marketing systems for small businesses. Social content, Google reviews, AI search and automations, all working together.

Why we exist

The problem we kept seeing.

Good businesses were losing to louder ones. Not because their work was worse. Because nobody had time to market it.

The owner would post for two weeks, get busy and go quiet for three months. Reviews sat without a reply. New leads waited days to hear back. From the outside, a great business looked closed.

More effort was never the fix. Owners are already working hard. The fix is a system that keeps going when you are busy. So that is what we build.

Today the places people look have grown. It is not just Google and Instagram. People ask ChatGPT who to call. That is why our work now covers AI search and automations too. Same goal as day one: make good businesses easy to find, easy to trust and easy to pick.
