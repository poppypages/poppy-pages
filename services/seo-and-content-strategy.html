What is AEO?

Answer engine optimization (AEO) is the work of getting your business named in AI answers from ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity and Copilot. It builds on good SEO: a site AI crawlers can read, clear answers to the questions buyers ask, an accurate Google Business Profile, matching listings and real reviews. How do AI tools decide who to recommend?

Each AI tool pulls from a search index and from sites it trusts. ChatGPT uses its own crawler plus partners such as Bing. Copilot uses Bing. Google's AI uses Google's index and Business Profiles. Perplexity runs its own crawler. So the basics matter in every one of them:

  • Can the AI read your site? Many AI crawlers do not run JavaScript. If your key pages only appear after scripts run, the AI sees an empty page.
  • Do your pages answer real questions? Clear, direct answers about what you do, who it is for, what it costs and where you work are easy for AI to quote.
  • Does the rest of the web agree? Your Google Business Profile, directories and social bios should use the same name, details and description.
  • Do real people vouch for you? Reviews and mentions on other sites carry a lot of weight in AI answers.

Common questions

What is the difference between SEO and AEO?

SEO helps you rank in search results. AEO helps you get named inside AI answers. They share the same base: a readable site, helpful pages, a strong Google profile and reviews. AEO adds a focus on clear answers and on what other sites say about you.

How long does AEO take?

Technical fixes can be picked up within weeks. Showing up in AI answers for competitive questions usually takes several months of steady work, because it depends on reviews and mentions that build over time.

Do I need llms.txt?

It is not a priority. No major AI company has said it uses llms.txt to pick what to cite, so we spend the time on things that are known to help.
