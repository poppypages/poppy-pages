Summary

Triangle Pediatrics is a pediatric practice in North Carolina. PoppyPages Marketing checks its Google reviews every day, drafts a reply for each new review for the office to approve, and runs its social media. The result: every new review gets a response, no negative review goes unseen, and the office gets more than 20 hours a month back.

The challenge

Triangle Pediatrics gives great care, but keeping up with its online reputation fell to front-desk staff who were already busy. Checking Google for new reviews and writing careful replies took hours, and it was easy for a review to sit unanswered.

What we set up

  • Daily review monitoring. Every day we check the practice's Google reviews against a running log, so any review without a reply is flagged.
  • Reply drafts for approval. Each new review gets a calm, warm reply draft that never confirms someone is a patient or mentions care details. The office approves it in one step.
  • Social media. We plan, design and post updates parents care about, like office news, seasonal reminders and staff spotlights.
  • Monthly reporting. A plain-English report on reviews, rating and replies.
