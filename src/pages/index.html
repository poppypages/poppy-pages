What does PoppyPages Marketing do?

We build and run marketing systems for small businesses. That covers social media content, Google review monitoring and reply drafts, AI search (AEO) and automations for leads, follow-ups and reports.

What is AEO?

AEO means answer engine optimization. It is the work of making your business easy for AI tools like ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity to find, understand and recommend. It builds on good SEO, clear answers on your website, an accurate Google profile and real reviews.

Do you use AI to do the work?

Yes, and a person checks all of it. AI helps us draft faster, watch reviews and build reports. Nothing goes live without a human review and your approval.

What can you automate?

Common ones are instant replies to new leads, follow-up emails, review requests and monthly reports. We start with the task that eats the most of your time.

Will the posts look like they came from us?

Yes. We start with your ideas, your tone and your audience. On our paid plans we match everything to your brand voice and style, and you approve the calendar before anything goes live.

How much time does my team need to spend on this?

Zero to 30 minutes a month. The free pack is ready to use. Our paid plans are done for you. We just need your approval on the calendar.

Is this safe? What if we get negative comments?

We monitor your Google reviews and social comments. We alert you right away to any risks and give you a draft reply so you can respond in a calm, professional way.

We have tried social media agencies before. How is this different?

Most agencies chase going viral. We build trust. We are not trying to make you an influencer. We make sure your business looks active, credible and open for business everywhere people look.

What platforms do you work with?

Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and Google Business Profile. Most clients start with one or two platforms and grow from there. We suggest starting where your customers already spend time.

How long before I see results?

Most clients notice a difference in the first two weeks. Your pages look active, professional and current. Growth in engagement and inquiries usually shows up within 60 to 90 days of steady posting.
