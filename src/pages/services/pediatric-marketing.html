Why reviews matter so much for practices

Choosing a doctor for a child is a big decision. Parents look at your Google rating, read the newest reviews and check whether the practice replies. A review that sits for weeks without an answer can say more than the review itself.

What we do for practices

  • Daily Google review checks. New reviews are flagged the same day, with negative ones marked so they get attention first.
  • Careful reply drafts. Each draft thanks the reviewer, stays general and invites them to call the office. It never confirms they are a patient or mentions care details.
  • Social posts parents find useful. Seasonal health reminders, office updates, staff spotlights and community events.
  • Comment watch. We flag comments that need a reply and tell you right away about anything sensitive.
  • A monthly reviews report. Your rating, review count and reply rate, month over month.

Common questions

Do you reply to reviews for us?

We draft every reply and your office approves it. You can post it yourself or have us post it, whichever you prefer.

Can you remove bad reviews?

No one can remove a real review just because it is negative. We can help you report reviews that break Google's rules and reply calmly to the rest.

What if a review mentions a medical issue?

The reply stays general and invites the parent to call the office. It never repeats or confirms health details.
