What makes a good small business website?

  • It loads fast on a phone. Most local searches happen on phones, so we design for small screens first.
  • It answers the big questions right away. What you do, who it is for, where you work, what it costs and how to reach you.
  • It has one clear next step. A call, a booking or a form, on every page.
  • Search engines and AI can read it. Real text in the page, clean headings, a sitemap and structured data.
  • It stays safe. Few moving parts, current software and security headers turned on.
