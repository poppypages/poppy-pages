Custom website or WordPress?

Both can work well. Here is how they compare for a local service business, so you can choose with your eyes open.

Get my free audit Book a strategy call

The short answer

For a small local service business with a handful of pages, a lean custom site is usually faster and needs less upkeep, because there are no plugins or database to keep updated. WordPress makes more sense if you publish a lot, need many plugins, or want lots of people editing the site. Either way, the content matters most: clear answers, fast pages and real text that search engines and AI tools can read.

Side by side

Lean custom siteWordPress site
SpeedUsually very fast, since pages are plain filesCan be fast, but page builders and plugins often slow it down
UpkeepLittle. No plugins to updateRegular updates to WordPress, the theme and each plugin
SecurityFewer moving parts to attackOut-of-date plugins are a common way sites get hacked
EditingSimple editing can be added for things like a blogEasy editing built in
Best forService businesses with a set of core pagesSites with lots of content, editors or features
AI searchText is in the page, easy for AI crawlers to readAlso fine, as long as key content is not loaded by scripts

Why we usually build custom

Our clients want a site that loads fast, answers questions and does not need babysitting. A lean custom build does that with the least upkeep. When a client really needs WordPress, we will say so.

Common questions

Is WordPress bad?

No. It powers a large share of the web and works well for many sites. It just needs regular updates and care, especially with lots of plugins.

Can I still have a blog on a custom site?

Yes. This website is custom built and has a blog with simple editing.
